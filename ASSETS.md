Original Kenney licenses are in `public/assets/licenses/`; Quaternius license evidence: https://quaternius.com/faq.html and the linked individual model pages. No attribution is legally required. No original model geometry is generated.

Material and scale normalization is applied at load time. Town modules form the cottage, coop and barns; nature models supply trees, bushes, flowers, crop beds, pond lilies, rocks, path and fences. Egg is used for pickups; cereal bowl for troughs; bee and parrot for flying wildlife. Animal models are reused for babies. Explicit per-instance mappings are stored in the renderer and audited in verification/REPORT.md.

Triangles count glTF indexed primitives once per source mesh (before scene instancing). External textures were downloaded from the same original pack archive. No topology was generated or replaced. The model loader uses SkeletonUtils clones, shared materials and original animation clips. Some architectural modules are retained as documented kit reserves and visible in the contact sheet rather than placed in the garden.
