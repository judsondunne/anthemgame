# Farm expansion

Cow pasture ← Chicken Garden → Horse meadow

- A continuous orthographic landscape with clamped, eased horizontal camera movement.
- Arrow keys, horizontal trackpad scrolling, on-screen navigation, and one-finger touch dragging.
- Cows graze; horses alternate grazing and running. Grain calls them to their pasture gate.
- Each pasture has a matching cedar/red barn; animals return there at night.
- Collectible eggs around the coop. Care and eggs award XP with flying particles and a smoothly filled level bar.
- One baby of each species may arrive periodically; babies follow adults and grow over time. Calm population caps.
- Persist progression locally. Validate controls, XP, feeding, eggs, night shelter, growth, and responsive screenshots before production deployment.
